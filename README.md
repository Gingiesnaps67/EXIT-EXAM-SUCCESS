# HESI Exit Command Center

A phone friendly study app for HESI exit exam practice. Open the published site at https://gingiesnaps67.github.io/EXIT-EXAM-SUCCESS/ .

## Add it to your phone

- **iPhone:** Open the site in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** Open the site in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- To share it, send the site link to a friend. They will have their own progress on their device.

The app is a static website. It does not require a paid app store or installation package. After the first complete visit, the app's core files are cached for offline use; Google Fonts may need a connection. Progress is saved in this browser's local storage. It does not automatically transfer between devices or sync with a friend's progress. Clearing browser data can erase it. The original app's optional Claude account sync is unavailable on GitHub Pages.

## Publishing

In the repository's **Settings → Pages**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, then **main** and **/(root)**, and save. The site URL is https://gingiesnaps67.github.io/EXIT-EXAM-SUCCESS/ . All web files, including `index.html`, must remain at the repository root. No build step is needed.

## Content

The app is an educational study aid, not for clinical decisions. Its scoring is not Elsevier's HESI scoring algorithm and does not predict an official score. Before sharing broadly, review the question text and source references for accuracy and permission to publish.
